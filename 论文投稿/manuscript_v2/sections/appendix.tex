\section{Grassmann and Test-Bed Details}\label{app:architecture}

The Grassmann branch uses causal local pairs, not future-token pairs. For a token embedding $h_t\in\mathbb{R}^d$, define the reduced representation $z_t=W_{\mathrm{red}}h_t+b_{\mathrm{red}}\in\mathbb{R}^r$. With $\mathcal{D}_t=\{\Delta\in\{1,2,4\}:t-\Delta\geq0\}$, the local direction is $z_{t-\Delta}\rightarrow z_t$. The coordinates of the exterior product are
\begin{equation}
p_{ij,t}^{(\Delta)}
=z_{t,i}z_{t-\Delta,j}-z_{t,j}z_{t-\Delta,i},
\qquad i<j.
\end{equation}
There are $r(r-1)/2$ coordinates. The subspace background follows the Grassmann interpretation of basis equivalence~\citep{edelman1998geometry}; the exterior-product interpretation of Pl\"ucker coordinates is documented by the authors of the geometric-algebra reference.\footnote{\url{https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~smann/GA/plucker_coordinates.html}. This author-maintained reference is used only for the exterior-product background, not as evidence about KD.}
When the two vectors are dependent, the exterior product is zero; the implementation remains finite through the normalization
\begin{equation}
\widehat p_t^{(\Delta)}
=\frac{p_t^{(\Delta)}}{\max(\|p_t^{(\Delta)}\|_2,\epsilon)},
\qquad \epsilon=10^{-8}.
\end{equation}
Geometric projection precedes the valid-window mean:
\begin{equation}
g_t^{(\Delta)}=W_{\mathrm{plu}}\widehat p_t^{(\Delta)}+b_{\mathrm{plu}},
\qquad
g_t=\frac{\sum_{\Delta\in\mathcal{D}_t}g_t^{(\Delta)}}
{\max(1,|\mathcal{D}_t|)}.
\end{equation}
An empty window gives zero. Mixing uses a feature-dependent vector gate, not a scalar average:
\begin{equation}
a_t=\sigma(W_{\mathrm{gate}}[h_t;g_t]+b_{\mathrm{gate}}),
\qquad
h_t^{\mathrm{mix}}=a_t\odot h_t+(1-a_t)\odot g_t.
\end{equation}
The implementation includes LayerNorm, dropout, and residual feed-forward processing around this core. The LM head produces G logits. The Transformer branch produces T logits independently; scalar $\alpha$ acts only at late-logit fusion (Equation~\ref{eq:teacher-fusion}). Figure~\ref{fig:teacher_architecture} describes this generic teacher and does not fix $\alpha=0.5$.

The compact primary student is recorded internally as \texttt{hybrid\_lite}. It uses six layers, width 224, eight Transformer heads, reduced width 56, dropout 0.1, and final-block fusion (\texttt{late\_k}=1). The selected student has 31,434,257 parameters. The local GPT-2 tokenizer has vocabulary size 50,257 and no added special tokens. Teacher dimensions and states are loaded from the frozen source configurations rather than inferred from student dimensions.

\section{Local Gradient and Curvature Diagnostics}\label{app:local-diagnostics}

The archived Phase 3A analysis comprises seven families and 21 dependent seed-level conditions. Training probes use the first 32 chunks, accumulated in FP32 microbatches of two over all 31.43M student parameters. Calibration seeds 314159, 271828, and 161803 each select two disjoint validation chunks, producing 63 calibration rows. Scores were frozen before endpoint merging. No test data enter the probes and no parameter updates are retained.

Let $g=\nabla\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CE}}$, $k=\nabla\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{distill}}$, $v=\nabla\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{val}}$, and $H_{\mathrm{val}}=\nabla^2\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{val}}$ at $S_0$. Write $a=v^\top k$, $b=g^\top H_{\mathrm{val}}k$, and $c=k^\top H_{\mathrm{val}}k$. The nominal local gain approximation is
\begin{equation}
\widehat G_{\mathrm{local}}(\lambda)
=\eta\lambda a-\eta^2\lambda b-\tfrac12\eta^2\lambda^2c,
\qquad \eta=10^{-4}.
\end{equation}
This is a local Taylor diagnostic, not a bound on the validation-selected test endpoint. All gradients and HVPs are finite. First- and second-order signs agree in 63/63 rows. Their seed-level AUROC is 0.644444 and sign accuracy 0.571429; family-mean AUROC is 0.600000. Both miss positive WT2 J, G, and TT families. Only 11/21 conditions have stable quadratic signs across all calibration subsets.

The median absolute curvature correction is $2.73\times10^{-7}$, or 0.52\% of the first-order magnitude; its largest relative contribution is 20.9\% near a small first-order term. It never changes a sign. A finite positive candidate $\lambda_{\mathrm{safe}}$ exists in 22/63 rows, ranging from 56.44 to 6429.64; 41 rows have no positive interval. These are diagnostic crossings, not recommended coefficients or verified safe training settings.

The literal first scheduler step has learning rate zero and is a no-op for every coefficient. This does not demonstrate safety. A separate nominal-rate clipped AdamW virtual step gives AUROC 0.700000, sign accuracy 0.571429, and Spearman correlation 0.427273; it misses 9/15 beneficial outcomes and has stable signs in 10/21 conditions. Table~\ref{tab:full_predictors} gives the full seed-level comparison. Calibration rows and families are dependent; these scores are not universal classifiers.
