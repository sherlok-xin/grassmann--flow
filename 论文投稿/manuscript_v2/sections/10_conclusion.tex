\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Matched warm-start continuations show reproducible positive transfer on PTB and WikiText-2 and negative transfer on TinyStories. At fixed composition, changing the trained teacher state reverses transfer, showing that state and predictive quality strongly modulate the outcome. A globally weaker source nevertheless improves all three students, so teacher likelihood alone is insufficient. Finally, ensemble supervision improves transfer, but the heterogeneous Transformer--Grassmann teacher has no advantage over the homogeneous control. These bounded findings support configuration-dependent evaluation rather than a geometry-specific mechanism or a general teacher-selection rule.
