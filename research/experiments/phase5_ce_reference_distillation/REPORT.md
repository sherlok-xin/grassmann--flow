# Phase 5 — live evidence report

Status on 2026-11-26: both legacy-domain short screens have completed and full pilots are running. This is **not a completed-method report**. Completed rows in `RESULTS.csv` distinguish smoke, short screen and full runs; `SCREENING_AND_PILOT_STATUS.md` and its CSVs distinguish completed endpoints from live progress. No Phase 5 test endpoint has been evaluated. Historical ARR scores are 2/2/1; historical manuscripts and final evidence remain protected.

The temperature-one projection and its exact adaptive CE/KD gradient equivalence are implemented and tested. The current-student reference control uses an extra preupdate eval-mode forward, matching the probability-extraction mode of the independent CE cache. Separate controls remove token-specific weighting, replace the reference with S0 or the full independent CE distribution, and permute non-target identities. Their implementation is not evidence that they succeed. `METHOD.md` states the mathematical boundaries and `PRIOR_ART_MATRIX.md` identifies existing target correction, scheduling and validation-feedback work.

All 23 one-epoch WT2 screens have completed. The selected ordinary KD configuration is lr=1e-4, lambda=5, T=2, with trained dev NLL 4.266468. The selected candidate A uses lr=1e-4, lambda=5, T=1 and obtains 4.283045, so A does not beat tuned KD in this short screen. Selected ATKD/W-Clip/ToDi obtain 4.282010/4.270367/4.297731. Candidate A, ATKD and W-Clip at their selected strengths have clip fraction 1.0 and retain `CLIP_SATURATION_WARNING`; all screen summaries report finite gradients. This does not establish clipping as the cause of the ranking. Full pilots and controls continue without changing coefficients or clip thresholds.

All six WT2 CE short screens select S0 (dev NLL 4.348015). The promoted complete ten-epoch CE run at lr=1e-6 also selects S0, with final trained dev NLL 4.356329. The separate matching CE reference at lr=1e-4 selects S0 and ends at 4.422212. These two tested full trajectories do not establish held-out CE improvement; the ongoing reference is still the actual independent CE trajectory, not a guarantee that its dev likelihood improves. The complete KD/A/baseline results must be interpreted against this limitation and the planned effective-weight/source controls.

TinyStories CE/KD/A eight-update smokes and all 12 short screens have completed. Its promoted CE uses lr=1e-4 and reaches short-screen dev NLL 1.370287 from S0=1.384469.
