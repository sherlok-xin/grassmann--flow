# Phase 5 — live evidence report

Status on 2026-11-25: implementation and bounded screening are in progress. This is **not a completed-method report**. Completed rows in `RESULTS.csv` distinguish smoke, short screen and full runs. No Phase 5 test endpoint has been evaluated. Historical ARR scores are 2/2/1; historical manuscripts and final evidence remain protected.

The temperature-one projection and its exact adaptive CE/KD gradient equivalence are implemented and tested. The current-student reference control uses an extra preupdate eval-mode forward, matching the probability-extraction mode of the independent CE cache. Separate controls remove token-specific weighting, replace the reference with S0 or the full independent CE distribution, and permute non-target identities. Their implementation is not evidence that they succeed. `METHOD.md` states the mathematical boundaries and `PRIOR_ART_MATRIX.md` identifies existing target correction, scheduling and validation-feedback work.

Six one-epoch WT2 CE screens have completed. Their trained dev NLL values at learning rates 1e-6, 5e-6, 1e-5, 2e-5, 5e-5 and 1e-4 are 4.348807, 4.353360, 4.357658, 4.363602, 4.369866 and 4.373888. These are exploratory short-budget results, not final ten-epoch effects or evidence of a successful new method. The preregistered full promotions and baseline/ablation matrix remain pending.
