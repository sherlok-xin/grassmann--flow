# Independent CE reference target correction (candidate, not a validated method)

Phase 5 is authorized on 2026-11-25. Historical conclusions and artifacts remain unchanged. At update t, an independently optimized CE reference R_t starts from the same S0 and uses the same examples, update count, optimizer initialization and schedule as the continuation student. Its training never receives KD. The reference probability is measured in eval mode immediately BEFORE its update; the additional forward is charged. Neither the final CE state nor a future reference state is used retrospectively.

For temperature one, write t=p_T(y), r=p_R(y). Minimizing KL(q||p_T) subject to q_y>=r gives q_y=max(t,r) and q_v=(1-q_y)p_T(v)/(1-t), v!=y. For a fixed gold mass, the KL chain rule minimizes the remaining conditional divergence at the original teacher non-target distribution; the binary KL is minimized at t when feasible, otherwise at the boundary r. Thus beta=max(0,(r-t)/(1-t)) and q=beta delta_y+(1-beta)p_T. Finite logits have 0<t<1 in exact arithmetic; implementation computes the tail directly with masked logsumexp to avoid subtracting a rounded probability from one.

With detached targets and beta, CE+lambda KL(q||p_S) has the SAME STUDENT GRADIENT as (1+lambda beta) CE+lambda(1-beta) KL(p_T||p_S). Scalar losses differ by a target-only entropy term. This is adaptive CE/KD weighting, not a new projection theorem. The prospective question is whether the independent improving CE trajectory supplies information unavailable to a current student, fixed S0, or mean weight. The target satisfies -log q_y<=-log r and preserves teacher non-target conditional ratios. Neither property guarantees student endpoint likelihood, generalization, or long-horizon safety. These statements are NOT extended to temperature two.

The non-target permutation control preserves gold mass and the full probability multiset, hence entropy, while changing non-target identities. It does not preserve gradient/update norm. Mean-beta uses the mean over ALL valid tokens in the optimizer batch, not a mean of uneven microbatches. Reference-only self-distillation needs the full reference distribution and cannot be recovered from a scalar gold cache.

Real AdamW forks retain moments, scheduler, RNG, cursor and accumulation state. Their CE comparison starts at the current fork state and is distinct from the independent trajectory from S0. Short-horizon control gain does not imply improvement over a complete CE continuation. All discarded branches, reference work, probes, evaluations and retries count toward total GPU hours.
