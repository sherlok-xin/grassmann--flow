# Final Evidence Ledger

## Scope and decision rules

This ledger is the authoritative claim boundary after Phase 2G. Positive KD gain is defined as `NLL_WS_CE - NLL_WS_KD`; positive teacher residual advantage is `NLL_WS_CE - NLL_teacher`. Unless stated otherwise, confirmatory KD results use token-mean KL, `lambda=5`, temperature 2, seed-matched independently trained S0 checkpoints, validation-NLL checkpoint selection, and test-only final reporting.

Evidence status has the following meaning. `CONFIRMED` denotes a matched result with consistent direction over three independent student seeds. `PARTIAL` denotes a reproducible or controlled observation with a material unresolved confound. `CONTRADICTED` denotes a proposition for which the frozen controls give evidence in the opposite direction. `EXPLORATORY` denotes diagnostic, single-seed, legacy, or unmatched evidence. “Allowed in main paper” refers to the exact conservative wording in this ledger, not a stronger causal interpretation.

## Non-negotiable exclusions

The main manuscript must not claim a Grassmann-specific KD advantage, causal benefit from Plücker geometry, superiority of architectural heterogeneity over homogeneous ensembles, a strong branch-disagreement mechanism, a successful CRBD method, a universal residual-quality sign law, or a unique Grassmann efficiency advantage. CodeParrot and old coefficient sweeps must not be mixed into confirmatory multi-seed tables.
